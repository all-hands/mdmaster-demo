# Thistlewick ferry timetable

A guide to the ferry between Thistlewick Island and Port Quillander.

## Tickets

Buy a ticket at the harbour office and keep it in your `pocket`.

## Sailings

Ask at the office for the `winter` sheet to see the times.

### Tides

The `--low-tide` note marks sailings that may leave late.
