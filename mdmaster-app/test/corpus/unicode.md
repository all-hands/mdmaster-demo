# Unicode

Prose is carried through unchanged: 3 × 4 … done, naïve café, Grüße.

Punctuation Word likes to substitute — em dashes, "curly quotes", 'apostrophes'
— survives the round trip.

- Greek: αβγ
- Cyrillic: Привет
- CJK: 日本語
- Emoji: 📄 ✅

> A quotation carrying a degree sign (21 °C) and a section mark (§ 3).
