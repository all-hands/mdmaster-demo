# Fogberry Jam

A file inside a folder: the folder comes back in the zip.

## Ingredients

- 1 kg fogberries, picked blindfolded
- 600 g moon sugar
- a squeeze of lemon

## Method

### Soften the berries

Whisper to them over a low heat.

### Boil

Rolling boil for eleven minutes.

## Storage

Keeps for a year, or until somebody finds it.
