# The Lighthouse Keeper's Handbook

Everything a new keeper on Thistlewick Island needs to know, in the order they
will need to know it. Compiled by Head Keeper Odalys Brambleshaw.

## Your first night

Arrive before the fog does. The fog is usually on time.

### Lighting the lamp

1. Wind the clockwork forty-one turns.
2. Ask the lamp politely.
3. Trim the wick.

#### When the lamp refuses

Read it a short poem. Limericks work best.

### The night log

Write down every ship, every whale and every ship that turned out to be a whale.

## Daily duties

### Polishing the great lens

Clockwise before noon, anticlockwise after.

### Counting the steps

There are 212 going up and 213 coming down. Nobody has solved this.

## Visitors

### The ferry

See the ferry guide for sailing days.

### School groups

At most twelve children and one goat per staircase.

## Emergencies

### Fog inside the tower

Open a window and wait for it to leave.

### The lamp is singing

Join in. It stops when it feels heard.
